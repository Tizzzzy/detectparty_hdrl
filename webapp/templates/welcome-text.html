Republican or Democrat?

In this experiment, you will be presented with a series of short political speeches. Each speech was given by a member of either the Republican or the Democratic party. We ask you to read each speech carefully and share whether you believe it was given by a Republican or a Democrat. An "explanation hint" will be shown alongside each speech to help you decide.

Informed Consent

This research is conducted by Northwestern University's Computer Science department. All data for research is collected anonymously for research purposes. Participation is voluntary, and you may only participate if you are 18 years of age or older. For questions, please contact Jessica Hullman (Ginni Rometty Professor, Computer Science Department) at jhullman@northwestern.edu.
