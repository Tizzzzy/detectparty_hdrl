Republican or Democrat?

Can you tell which party a political speech came from? How well can people predict whether a speech was given by a Republican or a Democrat based on its content, tone, and word choice? This is a research project hosted at Northwestern University by researchers in the Computer Science Department.

Research Team

Dong Shu, Ziyang Guo, Negar Kamali, Matt Groh, Jessica Hullman
