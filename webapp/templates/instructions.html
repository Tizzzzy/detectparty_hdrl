Republican or Democrat?

In this "Republican or Democrat" experiment, brought to you by Northwestern University's Department of Computer Science, you will be presented with a series of short political speeches. Each speech was given by a member of either the Republican or the Democratic party. Our aim is to understand how well people can tell which party a speech came from, and to evaluate how AI-generated explanations impact this prediction process. We ask you to read each speech carefully and share whether you believe it was given by a Republican or a Democrat. During the experiment, you may also be provided with an AI-generated explanation hint to assist your analysis.

Informed Consent

This research is conducted by Northwestern University's Department of Computer Science. All data is collected anonymously for research purposes. Participation is voluntary, and you may only participate if you are 18 years of age or older. For questions, see more on Informed Consent or please contact Professor Jessica Hullman at jessica.hullman@northwestern.edu.
